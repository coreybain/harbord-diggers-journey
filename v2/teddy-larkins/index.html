The story

From the paddock to your plate.

Whether you're in the market for a succulent rib-eye steak or a tender rack of ribs from the fields of country New South Wales, Teddy Larkin's has something to satisfy those carnivorous cravings.

Sourcing the highest quality ingredients from Aussie paddocks and the coast is at the heart of the menu. Filled with familiar favourites, it was designed to bring everyone around the table together.
