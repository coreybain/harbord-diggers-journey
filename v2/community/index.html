Est. 1930 Freshwater

Our story

Founded on community and mateship.

With friendships forged on the poppy fields of France, a group of Aussie Diggers returned home to the Northern Beaches wanting a place to meet and share their stories over a cold beer.

On 5 June 1930, Harbord Diggers was born.

Eight decades and counting

From a hut to the headland.

The Diggers story 1930 Harbord Diggers is born
  1. 5 June 1930

    Harbord Diggers is born

    Members met at an old hut by Freshwater beach. Billy teas were served to holiday makers, with the money raised going to members and families in hardship.

  2. 1933

    A storm takes the hut

    A storm washed away the hut, and members moved to a club member's residence.

  3. 1935

    The old barber shop

    The club relocated to Wilsons Old Barber Shop on Moore Road.

  4. 1952

    A home on the headland

    With the help of community leaders, the club lobbied for the headland where it stands today, and building commenced.

  5. Late 1960s and 70s

    Stars in the auditorium

    The club expanded, and stars from around the world played the auditorium to massive crowds.

  6. Late 1980s

    An Australian icon

    By the late 1980s, Harbord Diggers was one of the most iconic clubs in Australia.

  7. 2006

    Mounties Group steps in

    Mounties Group stepped in with a vision to revamp the club.

  8. 2014

    Approval for a new venue

    Development approval was granted for a new venue.

  9. 2016

    Construction begins

    Work started on the new club.

  10. 30 June 2018

    The new Harbord Diggers opens

    Stage One of the new Harbord Diggers opened its doors.

  11. 2019

    Stage Two completes the precinct

    Fitness, aquatic and the Watermark Freshwater retirement residences were completed.
