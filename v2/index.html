About us

Supporting the Northern Beaches community since 1930.

Harbord Diggers is a community-centred club, using our success to help improve members' lives and the Northern Beaches community.

Our award-winning Freshwater venue features bars, restaurants, entertainment and a spectacular Ocean Terrace — plus fitness, aquatic centres and kids' play at Major Fun across the precinct.
