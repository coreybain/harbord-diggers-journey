Native gardens, sweeping views.

Key terms

Good to know before you book.

Booking
  • Event holders must be a current Mounties Group member.
  • Event spaces are 3-hour duration.
  • Community rooms can be booked hourly.
On the day
  • All outdoor bookings have a 9.30pm curfew.
  • Cakes are welcome, with no cakeage.
  • No external food.
  • Decorations are not provided, but you're welcome to bring your own.
Cancellations
  • Cancellations made in writing at least 48 hours before the event receive an 80% refund of the minimum spend.
  • There is no refund for cancellations within 48 hours.
Not permitted
  • No 18ths, bucks, hens or fancy dress parties.
  • Confetti and gender-reveal balloons are not permitted.
