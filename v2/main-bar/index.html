The bar

A true hub for the locals.

Handcrafted cocktails, a curated wine list, refreshing beers and delicious food, all in a relaxed coastal setting.

Add live acts every Friday through Sunday and rotating Beer of the Month specials, and it's easy to stay a while.

Your go-to for great drinks, great music and great company.

At the bar

Four good reasons to stay for another.

  • Happy hour, with $12 cocktails: Margarita, Aperol Spritz and Espresso Martini. Monday to Friday. Not available 22 December to 5 January.

  • Four drinks to share for $32.

  • For members: $1 off Great Northern schooners, $1 off Balter XPA schooners and $1 off house red wines.

  • Squealing Pig's Squealini, in zesty lemon or fuzzy peach.
