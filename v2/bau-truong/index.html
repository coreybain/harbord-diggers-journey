The story

From Cabramatta to the coast.

Direct from its humble roots in Cabramatta, Bau Truong brings authentic modern Vietnamese cooking to Harbord Diggers. It's a family-run eatery, and the closely guarded recipes trace back to the childhood of head chef and owner Bac Cang, who migrated from Vietnam.

The menu takes its cues from Vietnam's vibrant food culture, with an emphasis on fresh, locally sourced produce and whatever is in season.
