The terrace

Perched above the coastline.

The Ocean Terrace is designed for relaxed dining, with stunning views and a laid-back coastal atmosphere.

It's the perfect place to gather, unwind and enjoy great food in a shared, open-air setting.

How it works

Ordering is simple.

  1. 01

    Find your spot

    Outside reserved times, tables on the terrace are first come, first served.

  2. 02

    Order your way

    Place your order at DD Collective café, or scan the QR code at your table.

  3. 03

    Sit back

    Breathe in the sea air and enjoy the view while your food is delivered straight to you.
