The story

Direct from its humble roots in Cabramatta, now on the Northern Beaches.

Bau Truong is a family-run eatery serving authentic modern Vietnamese. The closely guarded recipes trace back to the childhood of head chef and owner Bac Cang, who migrated from Vietnam.

Family-runRecipes handed down from Bac Cang's childhood, and still closely guarded.
In seasonA menu drawn from Vietnam's vibrant food culture, built on fresh, locally sourced produce.
