Our story

Friendships forged on the poppy fields of France, brought home to the beach.

A group of Aussie Diggers returned to the Northern Beaches wanting a place to meet and share their stories over a cold beer. On 5 June 1930, Harbord Diggers was born.

1930 Harbord Diggers is born
19302019
  1. 5 June 1930

    A place to meet

    Returned Diggers wanted somewhere to gather and share their stories over a cold beer. Harbord Diggers was founded on community and mateship.

  2. The 1930s

    Billy teas by the beach

    Members met at an old hut by Freshwater beach. Billy teas were served to holiday makers, with the money raised going to members and families in hardship.

  3. 1933

    The storm takes the hut

    A storm washed the hut away, and members moved to a club member's residence.

  4. 1935

    Wilsons Old Barber Shop

    The club relocated to Wilsons Old Barber Shop on Moore Road.

  5. 1952

    A home on the headland

    With the help of community leaders, the club lobbied for the headland where it stands today, and building commenced.

  6. Late 1960s to the 1980s

    Stars in the auditorium

    The club expanded, and stars from around the world played the auditorium to massive crowds. By the late 1980s it was one of the most iconic clubs in Australia.

  7. 2006

    A vision to revamp

    Mounties Group stepped in with a vision to revamp the club.

  8. 2014 to 2016

    Approved, then under way

    Development approval for a new venue came in 2014. Construction began in 2016.

  9. 30 June 2018

    The new Harbord Diggers opens

    Stage One opened its doors above Freshwater Beach.

  10. 2019

    Stage Two completes the precinct

    Fitness, aquatic and the Watermark Freshwater retirement residences were completed.
