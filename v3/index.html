About us

Supporting the Northern Beaches community since 1930.

Harbord Diggers is a community-centred club, using our success to help improve members' lives and the Northern Beaches community. Members also access health and wellness services across the precinct — fitness, aquatic centres, kids' play at Major Fun, and more.
