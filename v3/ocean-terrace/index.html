The terrace

Gather, unwind and breathe in the sea air.

The Ocean Terrace is designed for relaxed dining with stunning views. It's the perfect place to gather, unwind and enjoy great food in a shared, open-air setting.

Three kitchens, one table

A little bit of everything.

The terrace brings together a selection from DD Collective, Teddy Larkin's Seafood & Steakhouse and Bau Truong. Ideal for grazing, sharing, or simply enjoying a little bit of everything.

How it works

Ordering is simple.

  1. Find a table

    Book ahead for 12.00pm, Thursday to Sunday. Otherwise it's first come, first served.

  2. Order your way

    Place your order at DD Collective café, or scan the QR code at your table.

  3. Sit back

    Breathe in the sea air and enjoy the view while your food is delivered straight to you.
