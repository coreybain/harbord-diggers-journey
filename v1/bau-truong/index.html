The story

Direct from its humble roots in Cabramatta, Bau Truong brings authentic modern Vietnamese cooking to Harbord Diggers on the Northern Beaches.

It's a family-run eatery. The closely guarded recipes trace back to the childhood of head chef and owner Bac Cang, who migrated from Vietnam.

The menu takes its cues from Vietnam's vibrant food culture, with an emphasis on fresh, locally sourced produce and whatever is in season.

On the menu

Three to start with.

  1. Crispy golden soft-shell crab, wrapped in a fresh rice paper roll.

  2. Pork belly slow-cooked in fish sauce, served with a side of mixed pickle.

  3. The quintessential pho: Vietnam's fragrant noodle soup.
