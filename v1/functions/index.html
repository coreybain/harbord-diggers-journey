Let's plan your day.

Send the details through, or call function bookings on (02) 9105 4888. A few things worth knowing first:

Who can book

Event holders must be a current Mounties Group member. Not a member yet?

Duration and curfew

Event spaces are hired for three hours, and community rooms can be booked hourly. All outdoor bookings have a 9.30pm curfew.

Cancellations

Cancellations made in writing at least 48 hours before the event receive an 80% refund of the minimum spend. There is no refund within 48 hours.

Food, cakes and decorations

Cakes are welcome and there is no cakeage. No external food. Decorations are not provided but you can bring your own; confetti and gender-reveal balloons are not permitted.

Events we can't host

No 18ths, bucks, hens or fancy dress parties.
