The setting

Perched above the coastline, the Ocean Terrace is designed for relaxed dining with stunning views and a laid-back coastal atmosphere.

It brings together a selection from DD Collective, Teddy Larkin's Seafood & Steakhouse and Bau Truong, so there's something for everyone.

Ideal for grazing, sharing, or simply enjoying a little bit of everything.
