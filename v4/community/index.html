Our history

Two huts and a big idea.

From the first rugby league and cricket teams to a club with dozens of sub clubs of its own.

It starts with sport1927

The first clubs form

Mount Pritchard's first sporting clubs, rugby league and cricket, are formed.

The ideaOver a beer

A workers-style club

A couple of blokes in a Cabramatta hotel have the idea of a club of their own. The first meetings are held in members' homes.

The money£300

Raised in debentures

Around $600. Tom McAuliffe offers land in Meadows Road in exchange for debentures.

The clubhouseTwo huts

Hauled by road from Herne Bay

Two ex-government huts are brought from Herne Bay, now Riverwood, onto 1.2 hectares to become the original clubhouse.

The setbackKnocked back

The first application is rejected

An application as 'Mt Pritchard Workers Club Limited' is refused, with opposition from hotels, sporting clubs and the authorities.

May1964

Incorporated

Mt Pritchard & District Community Club is incorporated, with members of the original cricket team on its first committee.

Four more years1968

The liquor licence arrives

It takes four more years of persistence before the club is granted its licence.

NowToday

Home to 30-plus sub clubs

An award-winning venue with six dining and restaurant options, thirteen bar outlets and a full-service health and fitness centre.

Every dollar goes back.

All of the profit we generate is used to improve services or facilities for our members and to improve the lives of our members and the community.
