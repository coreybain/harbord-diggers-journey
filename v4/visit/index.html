Good to know

What to wear

Dress is smart casual: clean, neat and tidy. The following are not permitted:

  • Provocative shorts, training apparel or bare feet
  • Rubber thongs after 8:30pm
  • Muscle shirts, tank tops, see-through, midriff or crop tops
  • Hats or head-wear, except for religious, cultural or medical reasons
  • Leotards or swimwear
  • Offensive or political slogans
  • Dirty or untidy clothing, including work overalls
  • Torn or ripped clothing, unless by design
  • Motorcycle club colours
What to bring

Members, bring your card. Guests need a driver's licence, passport or other acceptable ID to sign in at the door.

Coming with children

Members have access to Major Fun, the indoor children's play centre at Mounties. Children under 14 cannot travel on the courtesy bus. More for kids
