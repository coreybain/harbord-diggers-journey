Vietnamese restaurant

Feast on modern Vietnamese.

Direct from its humble roots in Cabramatta, Bau Truong is now at Mounties offering authentic Vietnamese dining in South West Sydney.

View the menu ↗ Call 0424 326 388

Open seven days from 11am

The family behind it

A family-run eatery using safely guarded recipes, traced back to the childhood of Vietnamese migrant and head chef and owner, Bac Cang.

Inspiration for the menu comes from Vietnam's vibrant food culture, with emphasis on the use of fresh locally sourced produce and in-season ingredients.

You will find Bau Truong adjacent to the Terrace Bar.
