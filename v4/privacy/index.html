Your privacy, in plain words.

A short summary of how Mount Pritchard & District Community Club Ltd looks after personal information. The full policy is the one that counts.

Privacy Act
1988 (Cth)

The short version

Mounties handles personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Read the full policy →

This page is part of a design concept and is not the live Mounties site. No information is collected here, and the forms on these pages send nothing.

What is collected

Details such as your name, address, occupation, date of birth, proof of age and contact details.

How it is collected
  • Membership forms and renewals
  • Competitions and promotions
  • Sign-in terminals at the door
  • Bookings
  • Cookies on the website
At the door and on the premises

Guests are asked for a driver's licence, passport or other acceptable ID, which may be scanned on entry. The premises are under video surveillance.

How it is kept safe

Staff are trained to keep personal information safe.

Seeing or correcting your details

You have the right to access the information held about you and to have it corrected. Call Member Services on (02) 9822 3555 or ask at reception.
